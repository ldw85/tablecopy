This far exceeds similarly-priced VPS options.

Setup Differences

  • Mac mini: Requires tunneling (like Cloudflare Tunnel) for public access
  • VPS: Offers direct public access

Privacy and Security Concerns with VPS

When OpenClaw acts as your personal AI assistant, it will inevitably gain access to highly sensitive information:

  • API Keys: GitHub, X (Twitter), email accounts, cloud services
  • Credentials: Banking, productivity tools, and social media accounts
  • Personal Data: Calendar, contacts, documents, and proprietary business information

Storing these secrets on a remote VPS means they exist on a third-party server outside your control. Even with security measures, the attack surface is inherently larger. On your Mac mini, you can integrate with 1Password to securely manage all credentials locally—giving OpenClaw access while keeping your secrets under your physical control.

Workflow Integration: Local vs Remote

OpenClaw isn't just a backend service—it's a personal assistant that needs to work alongside your daily workflow:

  • Mac mini as local workstation: You can open the desktop at any time, check OpenClaw's status, view files directly, and collaborate seamlessly. Need to review a document? Just open it. Want to verify an action? Check the screen.
  • VPS is a black box: Everything happens remotely. To view files or debug issues, you must SSH in or use file transfers. Want to check what OpenClaw is doing? No direct desktop access. Documents need to be uploaded/downloaded—adding friction to every task.

The Mac mini transforms OpenClaw from a remote service into a true local AI workstation that you can see, touch, and control at any moment.

Other Advantages of Mac mini

  • Silent operation
  • Local data security
  • ~50% resale value after 3 years

Recommendation

The author highly recommends the Mac mini M4 for users who:

  • Are willing to set up tunneling
  • Seek long-term stability
  • Want to run multiple local services
