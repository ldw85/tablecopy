Apple 2026 Spring Event Preview: What to Expect

Apple has announced a special "Apple Experience" event for March 4, 2026 in New York, London, and Shanghai. Here's everything we know so far.

Apple has announced a special "Apple Experience" event for March 4, 2026 in New York, London, and Shanghai. The expected star of the show is a new low-cost MacBook featuring the A18 Pro chip (iPhone chip) and vibrant color options.

Key Details

  • Display: 12.9-inch
  • Chip: A18 Pro (first consumer Mac with iPhone chip)
  • Body: Aluminum chassis
  • Starting Price: Under $1,000
  • Brand: "MacBook" (reviving classic branding)

Colors

The event invitation features a 3D Apple logo in yellow, green, and blue. Expected color options include:

  • Light Yellow
  • Light Green
  • Blue
  • Pink
  • Silver
  • Dark Gray

This makes it Apple's most colorful laptop since the iBook G3.

Market Strategy

This device targets education markets and budget-conscious consumers, competing with Chromebooks while leveraging Apple's ecosystem advantage and the A18 Pro's neural engine for AI tasks.

Sources: MacRumors, Bloomberg, analyst Ming-Chi Kuo predictions
